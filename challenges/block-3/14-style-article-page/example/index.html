Why the Box Model Matters

By Ada Lovelace · 5 min read

Every element on a web page is a rectangle. Understanding how its content, padding, border and margin fit together is the first step to controlling layout.

Four layers

  • Content — the text or image itself
  • Padding — space inside the border
  • Border — the visible edge
  • Margin — space outside the border

Set box-sizing: border-box once, and widths finally mean what you expect.

Takeaway

Once spacing is predictable, every other layout tool becomes easier to learn.
